How to use the Quoter

A quick guide to every feature
The short version: pick Roll-Off or MSW at the top, fill in the customer, add the hauling, rental / service fees and disposal items, check the tax, then Save quote. To send it, use Quote sheet (print or save a PDF) or Email (opens a ready-to-send message). For a job with choices, save each version and send them together — see “Several options” below.

CCustomer

Enter the name and address. Type the ZIP and the city/state fill in automatically. The Email field is used as the “To” when you email the quote. Tax class (Commercial / Residential) sets the Solid Waste Tax rate — the first time you add a taxable item, the app asks which one.

1Hauling

If you came from the Mileage Quoter with Create Quote, the haul fee is filled in. You can also type a Site Address and Haul fee by hand. To change a mileage-based fee (e.g. a donation), just edit the Haul fee — a note shows it was adjusted, and Reset to mileage puts it back. The ← Mileage Quoter button up top takes you back to calculate by distance.

2Daily rental

Pick the container size and number of days. Rental is taxed only when the ticket has Solid Waste Tax (see Taxes).

3Disposal

  • Add item: start typing in the box — it filters by description or code. Tap one, set the Qty, then Add.
  • By county: the Disposal county on the Hauling card (set from the job’s county when you come from the Mileage Quoter) decides which facility’s items are listed — that county’s codes plus the ones that apply anywhere. To run Becker waste through Perham, switch it to Otter Tail. Leave it on “all counties” to see everything, with a county tag on each.
  • What the customer sees: the county never shows on the quote — “CLEAN DEMO - OT” prints as Clean Demo. The internal code and description still show in small type on your screen so you know which facility you picked. Admins can set the customer wording per code in Settings (“Shows on quote as”).
  • Custom item: tap ＋ Custom item not in the list for a one-off charge — enter a description, rate, qty and tax type. It won’t change your saved code list.
  • Change a quantity or remove a line right in the list after adding.

MMSW quotes — temporary & permanent dumpsters and carts

The switch at the top picks the kind of quote. Roll-Off is the one-time job. MSW · Temporary is a front-load dumpster for a while: delivery, the service fee for the period, and disposal extras. MSW · Permanent is ongoing service: delivery (often waived) and the service fees — no disposal section. Coming from the Mileage Quoter’s MSW tab sets this for you.

  • Delivery & Hauling: the mileage app’s MSW rate (temporary or permanent). Tick No delivery charge when it’s included — it prints as “no charge”.
  • Service Period Fees: pick the billing period (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual), then type the container and pickup — “2 yd weekly”, “95 gal every other” — and Add. The list shows that period’s codes for the service county in bill-code order — the numbered service codes first, then the lettered ones (rental, recycling, cardboard, key, cart cleaning, extra dumps). Changing the period re-prices lines that have a code for it.
  • What the customer sees: two parts — one-time charges (delivery, extras, their tax) and service charges per period (fees, rent, recycling, their tax) — each with its own total. The big number on your screen is the per-period amount; the one-time total sits under it.
  • Taxes: Solid Waste Tax applies to everything except recycling and cardboard dumpster rental. Residential vs commercial still sets the rate.
  • Codes: admins keep the MSW code list in Settings — rate, billing period, county, tax, and the customer wording.

4Solid Waste & Environmental Tax

Taxes figure themselves out from the items:

  • Solid Waste Tax turns on automatically when there’s an MSW item, and applies to hauling + rental + MSW items. The breakdown shows each piece and the tax it adds.
  • Environmental Tax (per yard or per ton) is figured automatically from demolition items — nothing to enter.

✓Finishing a quote

  • Quote good for __ days (default 30) and an optional Label this option show on the sheet/email.
  • Save quote stores it in the cloud for everyone and gives it a quote number (Q-2026-0101, Q-2026-0102…) that shows on the sheet, in the email subject and in the Saved list. Re-saving a loaded quote updates it and keeps its number.
  • Quote sheet opens a printable quote — use its Print / Save as PDF button.
  • Email saves the quote (so it has a number), marks it Sent, and builds the message in your Gmail: the formatted quote — letterhead, quote number, itemized table — with your Gmail signature under it, addressed to the customer. Gmail opens on the draft; look it over and press Send. The first time, Google asks you to Allow the quoter to create drafts and read your signature — pick your work account. Pop-up blocked? Tap Open draft on the green bar.
  • Prefer the old way, or Google can’t be reached? Settings → Email sending → Open a blank Gmail message and copy the quote: Gmail opens blank with your signature and the quote is on your clipboard — press Ctrl+V.
  • New quote clears everything; Duplicate keeps the customer + address to start a variation (it gets its own number when saved).

📁Saved quotes & status

  • Search by customer, address, quote # or label; the status dropdown narrows the list to Draft, Sent, Accepted, Declined or Expired.
  • Each quote has a status: Draft (saved, not sent) → Sent (set automatically when you Email it, or pick it yourself if you printed or handed it over) → Accepted / Declined when the customer answers. Change it with the dropdown on the row.
  • A Sent quote past its “good for” days shows as Expired automatically. Re-sending it (Email again) restarts the clock.
  • Load reopens a quote on the main screen; Delete removes it for everyone.
  • ⬇ Export CSV downloads whatever is listed (after your search/filter) as a spreadsheet — totals, taxes, status, dates — that opens straight in Google Sheets or Excel.

≡Several options on one job

  • Build the first version, give it a Label (e.g. “20 yd haul-only”), and Save.
  • Duplicate, change it to the next version, relabel, and save again.
  • Open 📁 Saved, tick the options, then Quote sheet (one PDF with all options) or Email (one message with all options).

⚙Settings

  • Rates: daily rental, Solid Waste Tax % and Environmental Tax rates. Change them here and every new quote uses them; quotes already saved keep the rates they were priced with.
  • Hauling rates: the $ per mile and base fees the Mileage Quoter charges (Roll-Off, MSW temporary, MSW permanent). Save them here and the Mileage Quoter picks them up on its next refresh — no more editing its code.
  • Billing codes: turn a code on/off, change its rate, tax type, county facility or customer-facing name (“Shows on quote as”), or add a new code. Changes sync to the whole team.
  • Admins: only admins can change rates, codes and the admin list — everyone else sees them read-only. Admins can add or remove other admins here.
  • Email sending: if you keep several Gmail accounts open, set your work email here so the Email button uses the right one (saved on your device).

↯Handy extras

  • Copy (by Email) copies the whole quote to paste anywhere — formatted (letterhead + table) in Gmail or Google Docs, plain text in a plain-text box. In Saved, tick options and Copy grabs them all. Prefer plain text everywhere? Turn off Copy the quote formatted in Settings → Email sending.
  • Set your email signature in Settings → Email sending — it replaces the default sign-off at the bottom of your quote emails, and is saved to your login so it follows you to any computer.

✎Your Gmail signature & the logo

Your existing Gmail signature is used — nothing to set up. With Gmail drafts it’s read straight from your Gmail settings and placed under the quote. With the copy method, Gmail adds it to the blank message itself and you paste the quote above it with Ctrl+V.

Prefer a plain sign-off? Turn off Use my Gmail signature in Settings — the email then ends with the default company sign-off or your typed plain-text signature instead.

To add the company logo to your signature (optional, one time, in Gmail):

  • Gmail → gear ⚙ → See all settings → General → scroll to Signature → click your signature to edit it.
  • Click the Insert image icon → Web Address (URL) → paste https://melgard05.github.io/quoter/logo-sig.png → Select. Resize with Small / Medium / Large if needed.
  • Save Changes at the bottom. Done — the logo now shows on every email, including quotes.
  • Ideas: submit a request, then View / manage to see all of them and mark each Done or Cancel. A red “new” badge shows when ideas have come in since you last looked.

Found a bug or want something added? Use the Ideas & Change Log box at the bottom of the main screen — it goes to the whole team.
